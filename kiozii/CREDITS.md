## 3D Open World Architecture & Terrain
- **Modular Connected Mountain Terrain**  
  7 interconnected fantasy mountain regions: Green Highlands, Autumn Peaks, Rocky Canyon, Frozen Ridge, Crystal Mountains, Ember Summit, and The Final Summit.  
  Designed and authored for Kiozii.  
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
- **Third-Person SpringArm Camera & 3D Character Rig**  
  Custom procedural animation and camera collision avoidance system.  
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
